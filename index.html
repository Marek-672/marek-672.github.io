The problem

Heavy rigs don't play in real time in Maya, so checking your timing means playblasting again and again.

Without it

Change a key, playblast, wait, watch, change another key, playblast again. Every check costs a minute, so you check less often than you should.

With LivePlay

Change a key and keep working. A moment later the player shows the new timing at full speed, with sound. You never leave the scene.

How it works

A hidden second Maya follows your edits and re-renders only the frames they change, starting with the frame you're on.

Only what changed

Edit a key and only the affected frames are redrawn, so the update arrives in a fraction of a second, not a full playblast later.

Full speed, with sound

The player runs your shot at real speed with its audio, while you keep animating in Maya.

Your hotkeys

Step frames, step keys, play and scrub with the Maya hotkeys you already use. It stays in sync with the time slider.

Draw over it

Pen and eraser with tablet pressure, layers, onion skin and hold. Export your drawings to Maya's Blue Pencil.

Paper mode

Fade the picture back so your drawings read clearly, then step through them like a flip.

Safe by design

It never saves over your scene, and it tells you clearly when something needs your attention.

Install

No restarts, no setup files to edit.

  1. Download and unzip LivePlay.
  2. Drag install_bgcache.py into the Maya viewport and click Install.
  3. Click the new shelf button. Updates install the same way, without restarting Maya.
